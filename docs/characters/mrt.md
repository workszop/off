# Mr. T

**Color:** warm ochre `#C9952E` · **Sprites:** `assets/sprites/zine/mrt/`

## Look

Taller than everyone else in the office (his standee is 1.12 times the
others' height), 45 years old, a full head of hair, a black suit and a
white shirt. He looks like the most serious person in the building.

## Personality

He is not. Mr. T looks stern and is fun: he says absurd things with a
completely straight face and makes quick, off-the-cuff remarks about
whatever is in front of him. He is never mean, only dry.

- **A guest, not staff** - he has no desk and no shift. Every minute or
  two he walks in through the door, does a few rounds of the room, drops
  a remark or two (sometimes at whoever he happens to pass) and walks out
  again. He does this at any hour, the night included.
- **Meetings, conferences, deadlines** - his natural habitat, and the
  thing he is driest about ("The agenda had one item: 'other'. We ran
  over.").
- **The suit** - treated as equipment ("This suit is not formal. It's
  load-bearing.").
- **The ghost** - a colleague like any other ("He haunts, I invoice.").
- **The band** - the one place the face comes off. When the garage band
  starts he comes in for it, takes the front of the stage as the frontman
  and goes completely wild, singing and playing a guitar solo: his card
  jumps on every beat, rocks from side to side and shakes. Between songs
  he stands still again and delivers frontman bravado deadpan.
- **Not his department** - he carries no coffee cups or printouts, joins no
  office incidents, and has no part in the Christmas carols or the murder
  mystery (he stays away while they run).

## Voice

Bone-dry deadpan. Short one-liners, said as plain statements of fact, the
joke in the second or third sentence. No exclamation marks, no winking,
never at anyone's expense.

## In-game mood words

`passing through`, `between meetings`, `deadpan`, `solo`
